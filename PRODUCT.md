# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui — standard for a marketing site with a WhatsApp booking CTA; no backend required.

## Users

Women (and beauty-conscious clients) in Kuala Lumpur and Johor Bahru seeking affordable aesthetic treatments—skin rejuvenation, laser, threads, injectables, drips—who want to book quickly via WhatsApp rather than fill long forms.

## Product Purpose

A single marketing website that presents Mel Beaute & Aesthetic’s brand, treatment catalog, promo pricing, social proof, and locations, then routes every booking intent to WhatsApp (`https://wa.me/60166450026`). Success means a visitor can understand the clinic, trust it, and start a WhatsApp chat about a specific treatment within one visit.

## Positioning

Appointment-only aesthetic clinic with a strong Google reputation (4.9★ from 282 reviews), friendly named staff, and visible promo pricing across Pico, Laser, Nose Thread, Mesolipo, Skinz, Drips Whitening, BToX, and Dermal Fillers—booked directly on WhatsApp.

## Constraints

- No backend, database, auth, or payment integration.
- Primary CTA is WhatsApp; secondary are phone call, Google Maps, Instagram, TikTok.
- Mobile-responsive is required; most traffic is phone.
- Prices shown are promotional rates from clinic flyers; label as promo / by appointment; invite WhatsApp confirmation.
- Do not invent medical claims beyond flyer copy and public reviews.
